# Assessment Results and Follow-up

*Sample content for demonstration only. This is fictional information and is not medical advice.*

## When results are ready

Contoso Assurance (sample data) publishes results in the customer portal under Health, then My reports. Timing depends on the level:

- Basic: within 2 business days.
- Standard: within 5 business days, because blood tests are processed at a laboratory.
- Comprehensive: within 7 business days.

You receive an email and a text message when your report is ready. The message never includes result details, only a link to the portal.

## What the report contains

The report has a summary page, your lifestyle score, each measurement with a simple colour band (green for within the usual range, amber for slightly outside it, red for clearly outside it) and short explanations written for non-specialists. It ends with a list of suggested next steps.

## When a result is flagged

If any measurement is in the red band, a nurse from the Contoso health team calls you within 2 business days of the report being issued to explain the result. The nurse cannot diagnose or prescribe. Their advice is to see your own doctor, and they can send a copy of your report to your doctor if you give consent. You may also ask for a free 20-minute telephone follow-up with a nurse for amber results.

## Urgent findings

In rare cases, a result may suggest that you need prompt attention. We try to call you the same day. If we cannot reach you after 3 attempts over 24 hours, we send an urgent letter. If you ever feel seriously unwell, contact your local emergency services rather than waiting for a report.

## Follow-up services

Depending on your plan and results, you may be offered:

- A free repeat measurement after 6 weeks, for example for blood pressure.
- A wellness programme of 8 weekly coaching calls, free on Plus and Premier and priced at 80 on Essential.
- Access to a nutrition or smoking-cessation course, covered up to 150 per year on Premier.
- Referral to a specialist, which is covered under your normal outpatient benefits with a doctor's referral.

## Correcting errors

If you think a result or personal detail is wrong, use Report a problem in the portal. We review within 5 business days and issue a corrected report if needed. Blood results can be re-tested once at no cost within 30 days.

## Next assessment

The portal shows the date from which your next free assessment is available, normally 12 months after the previous one. Your report remains available in the portal for 7 years.
