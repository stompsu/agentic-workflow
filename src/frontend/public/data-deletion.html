How to delete your data in __APP_TITLE__

What is stored

We store your chat conversations (sessions and messages) linked to the account you signed in with.

How to delete it

  1. Open this site and sign in with the same provider you used before (Google or Facebook). Each provider is a separate account with its own chat history.
  2. Open the account menu and choose "Delete my data".
  3. Confirm. All your chat history is deleted immediately.

Automatic deletion

__RETENTION_TEXT__

Your sign-in account

"Delete my data" deletes your chat history only. It does not delete your sign-in account. __SUPPORT_CONTACT_TEXT__

Facebook

You can also remove this app's access in Facebook (Settings and privacy, Settings, Apps and websites, Remove). That only removes Facebook's permission; it does not delete the chat data stored here.
