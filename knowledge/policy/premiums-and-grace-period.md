# Premium Payments and Grace Period

*Sample content for demonstration only. Contoso Assurance is a fictional insurer and all terms are invented.*

## Payment options

Premiums at Contoso Assurance (sample data) can be paid monthly, quarterly or annually. Paying annually gives a 4 percent discount compared with twelve monthly payments. Accepted methods are direct debit, debit card and credit card. Bank transfers are accepted for annual payments only and must include your policy number as the reference.

## Due dates

The first premium is due on the policy start date. After that, the premium falls due on the same day of each month, quarter or year. We send a payment reminder 10 days before each due date by email and in the customer portal. If the due date falls on a weekend or public holiday, the payment is collected on the next business day, but cover is not affected.

## Grace period

If a premium is not received by its due date, your policy enters a grace period:

- Monthly payers: 15 days.
- Quarterly and annual payers: 30 days.

Cover continues during the grace period and claims for treatment during that time are payable. If the premium is still unpaid when the grace period ends, the policy lapses from the due date, and any claims for treatment after the due date are not payable. The outstanding premium for the grace period is deducted from any claim paid during it.

## Failed payments

If a direct debit or card payment fails, we retry once after 3 business days. A failed-payment fee of 5 is added after the second failure. You can pay manually in the customer portal at any time using the Pay now button.

## Reinstating a lapsed policy

A lapsed policy can be reinstated within 60 days of the lapse date by paying all overdue premiums. Reinstatement within 60 days restores continuous cover, but treatment received during the gap is not covered. After 60 days a new application is required, and waiting periods and pre-existing condition rules apply again.

## Premium changes

Premiums are reviewed once a year and any change takes effect at renewal. We give at least 45 days written notice of a premium increase. Premiums also depend on age band, which changes at ages 30, 40, 50 and 60, and on whether you added or removed riders. Claims history for an individual policyholder does not affect the premium of an individual policy.

## Refunds

If you overpay, the excess is credited to your next premium. You can ask for a refund of a credit balance above 20 through the customer portal; refunds are paid within 7 business days to the original payment method.
