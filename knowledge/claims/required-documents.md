# Required Documents for a Claim

*Sample content for demonstration only. Contoso Assurance is a fictional insurer and all terms are invented.*

## Documents needed for every claim

Every claim to Contoso Assurance (sample data) needs these items:

- An itemised invoice from the provider showing the provider name and address, the patient name, the date of each service, a description of each service and the amount charged.
- Proof of payment, such as a receipt or bank statement line, if you ask to be reimbursed.
- The completed claim form, which the portal fills in automatically for online claims.

Handwritten invoices are accepted only if they carry the provider stamp and signature.

## Additional documents by claim type

| Claim type | Extra documents |
|---|---|
| Hospital stay | Discharge summary, admission date and diagnosis code |
| Day surgery | Operative report and anaesthesia record |
| Prescription medicines | Original prescription and pharmacy receipt |
| Physiotherapy | Referral from a doctor, valid for 90 days, and a treatment plan |
| Mental health counselling | Referral from a doctor for the first session only |
| Accident | Accident description, date, place and names of any witnesses |
| Road traffic accident | Police report number, if one was created |

## Medical reports

If we need a medical report to decide a claim, we ask your doctor directly and pay the fee. We will always ask for your written consent first. Doctors usually reply in 10 business days, and the claim is paused until the report arrives. You can speed this up by asking your doctor to reply to our request.

## Language and translation

Documents in English are accepted as they are. Documents in other languages need a translation; for invoices under 500 a simple summary of the services written by you is enough. For larger claims, an official translation is required and we reimburse up to 60 of the translation cost.

## Currency

Invoices in foreign currency are converted at the exchange rate on the treatment date, using the published rate of the central bank. The converted amount is shown in the claim decision.

## Keeping originals

Keep the originals of all documents for 24 months after the claim decision. We may ask to see them for audit, and a claim can be reopened if we cannot verify a document.

## What happens if documents are missing

If something is missing, we send a request listing the exact items. You have 30 days to reply. If we receive nothing in 30 days, the claim is closed without payment, and you can reopen it within a further 60 days by sending the missing documents with the same claim reference.
