# Coverage Overview

*Sample content for demonstration only. Contoso Assurance is a fictional insurer and all figures are invented.*

## Plans at a glance

Contoso Assurance (sample data) offers three personal insurance plans: Essential, Plus and Premier. Every plan covers accidental injury, hospital stays and emergency transport. The plans differ in annual limits, deductibles and optional benefits.

| Plan | Annual limit | Annual deductible | Monthly premium (adult, age 30-39) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Essential | 50,000 | 500 | 89 |
| Plus | 150,000 | 250 | 142 |
| Premier | 400,000 | 0 | 219 |

All amounts in this article are in the policy currency shown on your schedule and are examples only.

## What is covered

All plans include the following benefits:

- Inpatient hospital care, up to 120 nights per policy year on Essential and 365 nights on Plus and Premier.
- Emergency ambulance transport, covered at 100 percent after the deductible.
- Outpatient specialist consultations, with a referral from a general practitioner.
- Prescription medicines listed on the Contoso formulary, with a co-payment of 10 percent on Essential and 5 percent on Plus and Premier.
- Day surgery, including anaesthesia and recovery-room charges.

Plus and Premier add physiotherapy (up to 12 sessions per year on Plus, 24 on Premier) and mental health counselling (up to 8 sessions per year on Plus, 20 on Premier).

## Optional riders

Riders can be added at the start of a policy or at renewal. Each rider has a 60-day waiting period before benefits can be claimed.

- Dental rider: covers two check-ups and one scale-and-polish per year, plus 50 percent of fillings up to 600 per year.
- Optical rider: one eye test per year and up to 200 towards glasses or contact lenses every 24 months.
- Maternity rider: waiting period of 10 months. Covers prenatal visits, delivery and 30 days of newborn care.

## Waiting periods

Accidental injury is covered from the policy start date. Illness is covered after a 30-day general waiting period. Pre-existing conditions that you declared on your application are reviewed individually; where accepted they are covered after 12 months of continuous cover.

## Where to find your limits

Your policy schedule lists your plan, deductible, riders and the remaining balance of each annual limit. The schedule is issued within 5 business days of the policy start date and can be downloaded in the customer portal under Documents. Limits reset on the policy anniversary date, not on 1 January.

## Changing your plan

You can move to a higher plan at any renewal with no new medical questions if you have held your current plan for at least 12 months. Moving to a lower plan can be requested at any time and takes effect on the next monthly premium date. Changes to riders always take effect at the next renewal.
