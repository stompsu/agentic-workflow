# What Is a Health Assessment

*Sample content for demonstration only. This is fictional information and is not medical advice.*

## Overview

A health assessment is a preventive check-up offered to customers of Contoso Assurance (sample data). It combines a questionnaire, simple measurements and a short consultation with a nurse or doctor to give you a picture of your general health and to highlight risk factors early. It is not a treatment and does not replace care from your own doctor.

## Assessment levels

| Level | Duration | Included | Eligible plans |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | 30 minutes | Questionnaire, height, weight, blood pressure, nurse consultation | All plans, once per year at no cost |
| Standard | 60 minutes | Basic plus blood tests (cholesterol, blood sugar, kidney and liver markers) and a doctor consultation | Plus and Premier, once per year at no cost; Essential at a price of 45 |
| Comprehensive | 120 minutes | Standard plus resting heart tracing, lung function test, vision and hearing screens | Premier, once every 2 years at no cost; others at a price of 160 |

## What is measured

The Basic assessment records your blood pressure, resting pulse, body mass index and waist measurement. The Standard level adds a fasting blood sample. Results are compared with general reference ranges, which are explained in plain language in your report.

## Who can take part

Any adult aged 18 or over who has held a policy for at least 30 days can book an assessment. Young people aged 12 to 17 can take a Basic assessment with a parent or guardian present. Assessments are not offered during pregnancy for the Comprehensive level.

## What an assessment is not

An assessment does not diagnose illness. If a measurement is outside the usual range, the report recommends you discuss it with your own doctor. It also does not affect your premium or your cover; see the privacy article for details.

## Health score

Your report includes a lifestyle score from 0 to 100 based on your questionnaire answers on activity, sleep, nutrition, smoking and alcohol. The score is for your own use. A score of 70 or above is described as Good, 40 to 69 as Fair and below 40 as Needs attention. The score is a simple educational summary and is not a medical measurement.

## Benefits for customers

Customers who complete an assessment each year earn a wellness reward of 20 credit towards their next annual premium, up to a total of 40 over two years. Rewards are applied automatically within 14 days of the report being issued.
