# Claim Processing Times and Status

*Sample content for demonstration only. Contoso Assurance is a fictional insurer and all terms are invented.*

## Standard processing times

Once Contoso Assurance (sample data) has all the required documents, claims are decided within these target times, counted in business days:

| Claim type | Target decision time |
|---|---|
| Outpatient and prescription claims under 500 | 3 business days |
| Standard outpatient and day surgery claims | 7 business days |
| Hospital stays | 10 business days |
| Claims needing a medical report | 20 business days after the report is received |
| Claims under investigation | Up to 45 business days |

After a decision, payment is made within 3 business days by bank transfer.

## Claim statuses

You can follow your claim in the customer portal under My claims. The status can be:

- Received: we have your claim and are checking it is complete.
- Waiting for information: we asked for more documents. The 30-day reply period applies.
- In assessment: a claims assessor is reviewing the claim.
- Approved: the claim is accepted in full.
- Partly approved: some items were declined or reduced. The decision letter gives the reason for each item.
- Declined: no benefit is payable. The letter names the policy clause.
- Paid: payment was sent. The portal shows the payment date and amount.
- Closed: no response to a request for information.

## Notifications

We send an email and a portal notification at every status change. You can also choose text message alerts for decisions and payments in Settings.

## Why a claim may take longer

Typical causes of delay are missing documents, a duplicate invoice check, a request for a medical report and claims arising in the first 60 days of a policy, which are always checked for pre-existing conditions. High volumes after public holidays can add up to 2 business days.

## Urgent claims

If a delay causes financial hardship, for example a hospital bill due within 7 days, call the claims line and ask for a priority review. Priority reviews are answered within 1 business day, although the decision itself still depends on having complete documents.

## Payment details

Payments are sent in the policy currency. Bank transfers normally arrive within 1 to 2 business days after payment is sent. Each payment comes with a statement that shows the invoice amount, the deductible applied, the co-payment, and the net amount paid. Deductible is applied once per policy year to the first eligible claims.
