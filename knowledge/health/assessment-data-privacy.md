# Privacy of Health Assessment Data

*Sample content for demonstration only. This is fictional information and is not legal or medical advice.*

## Our commitments

Health assessment data is among the most sensitive information Contoso Assurance (sample data) handles. We use it to deliver your assessment, to produce your report and to contact you about follow-up. We do not use it to set your premium, to decide whether to renew your policy or to assess a claim.

## Who can see your data

- You, through the customer portal after multi-factor sign-in.
- The clinical team at the partner clinic that carried out your assessment, for 90 days after the visit, to answer queries.
- The Contoso health team nurses who contact you about flagged results.
- Your own doctor, only if you give written consent for a specific report.

Claims staff and sales staff cannot open assessment reports. Access by clinical staff is recorded, and these records are reviewed every month.

## Where data is stored

Questionnaire answers, measurements and reports are stored separately from policy and claims records. They are encrypted in transit and at rest. Laboratory samples are labelled with a code instead of your name, and the laboratory does not receive your policy details.

## How long we keep it

Reports are kept for 7 years, so you can compare results over time. Raw laboratory data is deleted after 24 months. Questionnaire drafts that you start but do not submit are deleted after 30 days.

## Your rights

You can, at no charge:

- Download a copy of all your assessment data. We prepare it within 14 days.
- Ask us to correct data that is wrong.
- Ask us to delete your assessment data. We do so within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep a record, in which case we tell you the reason and the period.
- Withdraw consent for optional uses, such as anonymous statistics, at any time in Settings.

## Anonymous statistics

If you agree, an anonymised summary of your results, with no name, birth date or policy number, can be used for wellness research. Groups with fewer than 50 people are never reported. Opting out has no effect on your cover or your reward.

## Sharing with employers

If your assessment was arranged through your employer, your employer receives only the number of participants and general group trends, never individual results. An employer cannot ask us for an individual's report.

## Reporting a concern

If you think your data has been seen by someone who should not have it, contact the Data Protection Office through the message centre. We acknowledge within 1 business day and inform you of the outcome of our investigation within 30 days.
