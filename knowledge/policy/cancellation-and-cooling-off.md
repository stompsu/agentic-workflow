# Cancellation and Cooling-off Period

*Sample content for demonstration only. Contoso Assurance is a fictional insurer and all terms are invented.*

## Cooling-off period

When you buy a new policy from Contoso Assurance (sample data), you have a 14-day cooling-off period starting on the day you receive your policy documents. If you cancel within this period you receive a full refund of any premium paid, provided you have not made a claim. If you have made a claim, you are charged the premium for the days cover was in force, and no refund is made for the claimed amount.

## Cancelling after the cooling-off period

You can cancel at any time after the cooling-off period. To cancel, use the Cancel policy option in the customer portal, or send a written request through the secure message centre. Cancellation takes effect on the date you choose, which can be up to 30 days in the future, or on the date we receive your request if no date is given.

## Refunds on cancellation

- Annual payers receive a refund for the full unused months, minus a cancellation fee of 25.
- Quarterly payers receive a refund for full unused months in the current quarter, minus the same fee.
- Monthly payers receive no refund for the current month, and no further payments are collected.

Refunds are paid within 10 business days to the original payment method. If you cancel after a claim has been paid in the current policy year, the refund is reduced by any benefit paid that exceeds the premium collected so far.

## Cancellation by Contoso Assurance

We may cancel a policy only for these reasons: non-payment after the grace period, fraud or misrepresentation, or your move outside the covered territory. We send written notice at least 30 days before cancellation, except for non-payment, where notice is sent on the first day of the grace period. We do not cancel a policy because you made claims or because your health changed.

## Transferring and ending cover on death

A policy cannot be transferred to another person. If the policyholder dies, cover for named dependants continues for 90 days, and dependants can take over the policy as the new policyholder within that time without new medical questions.

## Cancelling riders only

Individual riders can be removed at renewal with 30 days notice. Removing a rider mid-year is not possible, but you can ask us to stop renewing it.

## Confirmation

After cancellation we send a confirmation letter within 3 business days. It states the end date, the refund amount and the date the refund was issued. Keep this letter if you need proof of continuous cover for a new insurer; we provide a free certificate of continuous cover within 5 business days on request.
