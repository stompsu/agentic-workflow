# Booking and Preparing for a Health Assessment

*Sample content for demonstration only. This is fictional information and is not medical advice.*

## How to book

Book a health assessment in the Contoso Assurance (sample data) customer portal under Health, then Book an assessment. Choose the level, a partner clinic and a time slot. You can also call the customer line from 08:00 to 18:00 on weekdays. Appointments are normally available within 14 days. Bookings are confirmed by email and text message right away.

## Clinics and mobile service

More than 120 partner clinics take part in the fictional network. Customers in rural areas can ask for a mobile assessment unit, which visits towns on a monthly schedule shown in the portal. Video consultations are available for the questionnaire and consultation parts of a Basic assessment, although measurements still require an in-person visit.

## Rescheduling and cancelling

You can change or cancel a booking free of charge up to 24 hours before the appointment. Cancelling later, or missing the appointment, uses up your free assessment for the year unless you have a documented emergency. Reschedule from the portal or by replying to the confirmation text with the word CHANGE.

## Preparing for a Standard or Comprehensive assessment

For the Standard and Comprehensive levels, which include blood tests:

- Do not eat or drink anything except water for 10 to 12 hours before the appointment. Morning appointments are easiest.
- Drink water normally, because it makes the blood sample easier to take.
- Avoid alcohol for 24 hours before and heavy exercise for 12 hours before.
- Continue taking prescribed medicines unless your own doctor tells you otherwise, and bring a list of them.

For all levels, bring photo identification, your policy number and any glasses or hearing aids you normally use.

## The questionnaire

You will receive a link to an online questionnaire 3 days before the appointment. It takes about 15 minutes and covers medical history, family history, lifestyle and current symptoms. Completing it in advance shortens the visit. If you cannot use the online form, a nurse can complete it with you at the clinic.

## Arrival and what to expect

Arrive 10 minutes early. A Basic assessment takes about 30 minutes in total. Wear loose clothing with sleeves that roll above the elbow. If you feel unwell on the day, with a fever for example, reschedule rather than attend.

## Accessibility

All partner clinics are step-free. Tell us when booking if you need a sign language interpreter, longer appointment or a quiet room. We arrange these at no cost with 5 business days notice.
