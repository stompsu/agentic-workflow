# How to File a Claim

*Sample content for demonstration only. Contoso Assurance is a fictional insurer and all terms are invented.*

## Ways to submit

Policyholders of Contoso Assurance (sample data) can submit a claim in three ways:

- Customer portal: choose New claim, select the person treated and upload your documents. This is the fastest route.
- Mobile upload: photograph each document in the portal's mobile view. Each file can be up to 10 MB, in PDF, JPG or PNG format.
- Post: send originals or copies to the Claims Processing Centre. Posted claims take about 5 business days longer because of scanning.

Telephone claims are not accepted, except for emergency hospital admissions, which can be notified on the 24-hour line.

## Deadlines

Submit a claim within 90 days of the treatment date. Claims received between 91 and 180 days after treatment are accepted only if you explain the delay, and may be reduced by up to 20 percent. Claims received more than 180 days after treatment are not payable.

## Hospital admissions

For planned hospital stays, ask for pre-authorisation at least 5 business days before admission. We reply within 2 business days. With pre-authorisation, the hospital can bill Contoso Assurance directly and you pay only the deductible and any co-payment. For emergency admissions, notify us within 48 hours or on the next business day.

## Claim reference

After you submit, you receive a claim reference in the format CL-2026-000000 by email within 1 hour for portal claims. Quote this reference in every message. Posted claims receive their reference once scanned.

## Direct payment or reimbursement

Choose who is paid when you file. If you paid the provider, we reimburse you by bank transfer. If the provider has not been paid, you can ask us to pay the provider directly, which is available for providers in the Contoso network only. Reimbursement goes to the bank account saved in the portal; changing the account requires a one-time code sent to your registered mobile number.

## Claiming for dependants

Policyholders may file for any dependant named on the policy. Adult dependants aged 18 or over can also register their own portal access, and their claim details are then visible only to them unless they grant access.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Uploading a card payment slip instead of an itemised invoice.
- Forgetting to select the correct person treated.
- Submitting the same invoice twice, which delays processing by up to 5 days while we check for duplicates.
- Blurred or cropped images; every page edge and date must be visible.
