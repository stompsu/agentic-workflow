# Appeals and Complaints About Claims

*Sample content for demonstration only. Contoso Assurance is a fictional insurer and all terms are invented.*

## When you can appeal

You can appeal any claim decision from Contoso Assurance (sample data) if you think the decision is wrong. This includes declined claims, partly approved claims, reduced amounts and claims closed without payment. You must appeal within 60 days of the date on the decision letter.

## How to appeal

1. Open the claim in the customer portal and choose Appeal this decision, or write through the secure message centre quoting the claim reference.
2. Explain why you disagree. State which item or clause you are challenging.
3. Attach any new information, such as a medical letter from your doctor.

You do not need a lawyer. Appeals are free.

## Stage one: internal review

A different assessor from the one who made the first decision reviews your appeal. We confirm receipt within 2 business days and decide within 20 business days. If a medical opinion is needed, the review may take up to 30 business days, and we tell you so in advance. The result explains the reasons and, if the decision is changed, the new amount and payment date.

## Stage two: senior panel

If you are still not satisfied, you can ask for a senior panel review within 30 days of the stage one result. The panel includes a medical officer who was not involved before. It decides within 30 business days. The panel decision is final within Contoso Assurance.

## External review

After the senior panel, you can take your case to the independent Insurance Ombudsman Service (a fictional body in this sample data). You must do so within 6 months of the panel decision. We provide the contact details in the decision letter. The ombudsman's decision is binding on us up to 150,000 if you accept it.

## Complaints about service

A complaint about service, for example delays or rude communication, is separate from an appeal about a decision. Complaints are acknowledged within 2 business days and answered within 10 business days. You can raise one by calling the customer line or by writing in the message centre.

## Tips for a strong appeal

- Quote the policy clause named in the decision letter.
- Include a short letter from your treating doctor if the issue is medical necessity.
- Keep it factual and list the dates of treatment.
- Appeals with new evidence are successful more often than appeals with only a re-statement of the claim; in the sample data set about 38 percent of appeals with new evidence were changed in the customer's favour.
