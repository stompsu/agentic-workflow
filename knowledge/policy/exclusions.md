# Exclusions and Limitations

*Sample content for demonstration only. Contoso Assurance is a fictional insurer and all terms are invented.*

## General exclusions

Contoso Assurance (sample data) does not pay benefits for any of the following, on any plan:

- Cosmetic procedures that are not needed to treat an injury or illness.
- Treatment that is experimental or not recognised by the national medical authority.
- Injuries caused by deliberate self-harm or by taking part in a criminal act.
- Treatment received outside the covered territory, except emergency care during trips of up to 45 days.
- Costs that are paid by another insurer, a government scheme or an employer scheme.
- Routine dental care, glasses and maternity care, unless you hold the matching rider.

## Hazardous activities

Injuries from the following activities are excluded unless you declared the activity and we accepted it in writing: motor racing, professional sport, mountaineering above 4,000 metres, skydiving, base jumping and scuba diving below 40 metres. Recreational activities such as hiking, cycling, skiing on marked pistes and swimming are covered.

## Pre-existing conditions

A pre-existing condition is any illness, symptom or injury for which you received advice or treatment in the 24 months before your policy started. If you did not declare it on your application, claims relating to it can be declined. If you declared it, we review it within 15 business days and tell you whether it is covered, covered with an additional premium of up to 25 percent, or excluded for a stated period.

## Sub-limits

Some benefits have lower limits than the plan's annual limit:

- Alternative therapies such as acupuncture: 8 sessions per year, maximum 60 per session.
- Hospital private room upgrade: up to 150 per night.
- Home nursing after discharge: up to 14 days per event.
- Hearing aids: one device per ear every 36 months, up to 800 per device.

## Fraud and misrepresentation

If a claim contains false or deliberately misleading information, we may decline it, cancel the policy and recover any amounts already paid. If an error on your application was innocent, we will adjust the terms from the date we find the error instead of cancelling.

## Reviewing a declined item

If a claim item is declined because of an exclusion, the decision letter names the exact clause. You can ask for a review by following the appeals process described in the claims section of the knowledge base. Reviews of exclusion decisions are completed within 20 business days.
